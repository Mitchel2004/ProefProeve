## About

This is a horror game made in Unreal Engine for our mock exam. We had 4 weeks (2 sprints) to make it.

You as the player have to escape from an 1800's English city where a porcelain doll is haunting you. You have to dwell in the narrow streets of the city to look for the key of the city gate. It is very foggy and you can't see much. Luckily you have a small lantern with you to see a little bit more around you. When the doll is near, you can hear heartbeats. Around the map there are apples you can collect to keep your stamina up. The more stamina you have the longer you can sprint.
